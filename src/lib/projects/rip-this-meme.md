A satirical cemetery for internet memes that have shuffled off this mortal coil. Each meme gets a tombstone with birth and death dates, cause of death, and an obituary written somewhere between eulogy and roast.

Visitors can interact three ways: Press F to Pay Respects, Press S to Throw Shade, or Press R to Resurrect — each with curated epitaphs you can sign with your initials and an emoji. Constrained expression. Community without chaos.

Built in four days with Nuxt 4, Strapi 5, and PostgreSQL, all containerized in Docker and self-hosted on a DigitalOcean droplet. The tombstone portraits are AI-generated (with background removal via rembg), and the whole thing is a love letter to the fleeting nature of internet culture.

Harambe is there. So is Dat Boi. And somewhere in the collection, there's a dog sitting in a burning room, telling himself everything is fine.
