The site you're looking at right now, where I post things I want to. Somethinf of a personal portfolio built with SvelteKit, featuring an interactive homepage where content floats around as physics-enabled blobs.

Projects and musings drift through a [Matter.js](https://www.brm.io/matter-js/) simulation, connected by spring constraints to their shared tags. Click a filter button and matching items glow while others fade. Drag things around. Watch them bounce off the walls.

Built with SvelteKit 2 and Svelte 5, using mdsvex for markdown content. The physics playground auto-sizes each blob to fit its title text, with balanced line breaks for longer names. Statically generated and deployed to GitHub Pages.

The whole thing is a love letter to the tactile web.  I wanted a personal site that felt like *mine*, not another template. Sometimes that means making things float for no reason other than it's fun to watch.  (Note: I did some similarly silly with [RipThis.Meme](https://ripthis.meme) with the fireflies and moon)
